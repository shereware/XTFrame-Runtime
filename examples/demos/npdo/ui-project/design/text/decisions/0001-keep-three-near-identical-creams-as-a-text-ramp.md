Context: design.audit reports near-duplicates #e8e2d4 / #efe8d8 / #f6f1e6 (dE2000 < 3) and #050505 / #0a0a0a.
Decision: do not merge them. They are used as a deliberate ramp: cream (#e8e2d4) default text, cream-accent (#efe8d8) rules and selected states, cream-bright (#f6f1e6) headings and hover labels. #050505 is the page ground, #0a0a0a the ink used on cream fills and under panel alpha.
Consequence: five swatches stay; merging would flatten the hover / selected contrast the screens rely on.
