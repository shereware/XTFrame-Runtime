# Principles

One heading per principle; each heading is a link target (`text:principles#<heading>`).

## Readable at a glance

The information a player needs right now reads in under a second at the smallest target resolution.

## Quiet until needed

Chrome is dim and translucent; screens fade in with staggered 500-1600 ms ease-out opacity timelines, so the background art and the game stay in front.

## Warm monochrome, one red

Near-black ground and one bone / cream ramp carry all type, rules and highlights. The only saturated colours are meaning: blood red for danger and for the single emphasised word (處分), sage for safe status. Never decorate with colour.

## Paperwork in the ruins

The player is a government officer on a site survey, not an explorer: the story bible makes the job, the agency and the word 處分 (disposal) the frame of the game. Chrome borrows from survey forms: ruled lines, labelled fields, small caps-like tracking, form-style headings, so the UI feels like an official instrument inside a decaying place.

## Dread first, defiance later

The game has two registers. Route 1 is survival (hold your breath, hide your fear): the UI stays quiet, dim and sparse, and nothing on screen is louder than the player's own fear. Route 2 turns the fear around ("the ghosts should fear you"): the same chrome may grow louder (warmer light, the one red used more boldly, punchier motion) but keeps its structure. Design every screen for Route 1 first; Route 2 is a variant of it, not a new look.

## Respect the safe area

Anchored elements sit inside the safe area (2.5 % default, 5 % console) on the 1280 x 720 logical canvas. Title and copy stay in the top-left and bottom-right corners, as in the key art; the centre belongs to the scene.

## Immediate feedback

Hover and selected states answer within 140-160 ms (ease-out) and always change contrast, not only colour.
