## Notes from third_party/NOTICE.md

### Vendored vs. fetched

- **Vendored** (source committed under `third_party/`, unmodified): doctest, stb, nlohmann/json,
  SheenBidi, libunibreak, bc7enc_rdo (bc7enc/bc7decomp/rgbcx). See `third_party/README.md` and each subdirectory's `VERSION.txt` for
  exact commit/tag and source URL.
- **Fetched at configure time** via CMake `FetchContent` (pinned tags, built from source, never
  installed system-wide): FreeType and HarfBuzz (`cmake/xt_text_deps.cmake`), SDL2
  (`cmake/xt_sdl2.cmake`), Clipper2 (`cmake/xt_clipper2.cmake`; only `Clipper2Lib`'s engine, offset
  and rectclip sources are compiled), cpp-httplib (`cmake/xt_httplib.cmake`, header only) and miniz
  (`cmake/xt_miniz.cmake`, only `miniz.c`).
- **Test-only assets** (not linked into any library or tool): the Noto font subsets under
  `tests/fonts/`, OFL-licensed; see `tests/fonts/README.md` for how they were produced and their
  per-file registered family names.

### Replacing the Qt libraries

You may replace the Qt libraries used by the editor with your own, modified or unmodified, build of
the same Qt major and minor version (binary compatible), and run the editor with them:

1. Build Qt from source (the archive above) with the same major.minor version, with your changes.
2. **Windows**: copy your `Qt*.dll` files and the `platforms/` (and other plugin) directories over
   the ones in the editor's installation directory, next to `xtframe-editor.exe`.
   **Linux (AppImage)**: extract the AppImage (`./XTFrame*.AppImage --appimage-extract`), replace
   the `libQt*.so*` files and plugin directories under `squashfs-root/usr/lib`, and run
   `squashfs-root/AppRun`. Alternatively set `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` / `QT_PLUGIN_PATH` to your build.
3. Start the editor. Nothing in the editor checks or blocks a replaced Qt library.

The XTFrame EULA does not restrict modification of the Qt libraries contained in the editor, or
reverse engineering of the editor for debugging such modifications, to the extent the LGPL v3
section 4 requires it.
