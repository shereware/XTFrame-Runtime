# Space and shape

Logical canvas 1280 x 720. Edges are square: 1 px hairline borders (`line-*` swatches), radius 3 px on key caps, full radius only on the settings switch.

## Rhythm

Gaps of 2, 4, 6, 10, 14 and 26 px are used; rows are 40 px, tabs 44 px, menu items 42 px, inventory slots 62 x 70 px. Screen edges keep 28-40 px of padding inside the safe area.
