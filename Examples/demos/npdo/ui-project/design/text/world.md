# World

Summary of the story bible (`ref:story/npdo-story-zh`, English: `ref:story/npdo-story-en`). The UI should feel like it belongs to this world; it does not tell the story.

## Premise

The player is an officer of the National Property Administration (國有財產署) who disposes of state land. The assignment: survey a hospital on land leased by a dissolved legal entity (built 1991, abandoned 1997) and decide how to dispose of it. The survey turns into imprisonment.

## The hospital

Two connected buildings: the Surgical Building (cardiac surgery) and the Internal Medicine & Psychosomatic Medicine Building (frontier treatments). Funded by a foundation; sealed since a failed hostage rescue twenty years ago.

## What is inside

Obsessions left by experiments on bio-energy (生體能量) run as a cult-like project for control; monsters twisted in mind and body; intruders assimilated and fused; and ghosts that were always there.

## Two routes

Route 1: survive. Hold your breath, hide your fear, evade, solve mechanisms, learn the past, get out. Route 2: fight back. "The ghosts should fear you."

## Open questions

- Hospital name and city: the UI shows "St. Ren Hospital / Taipei" (聖仁醫院), the story bible names neither.
- Dates: abandoned 1997, incident "twenty years ago": when is the game set?
- Is Route 2 a separate route, a later phase or a mode? Item text changes with it.
