# Imagery

## Key art

Two reference key arts set the look: the exterior of St. Ren Hospital at dusk (`ref:key-art/af3f1d8f-b44b-402c-8232-d975d9c177a8`) and a ward corridor with a lit doorway (`ref:key-art/d5b9776d-3c07-4237-835b-f79ffc258b9c`).

- Photoreal, desaturated, wet and overgrown; near-black shadows (about 35 % of the exterior is #060605, another 17 % #161715), cold blue-grey with one warm light source.
- Foreground is dark and out of focus (clipboard, rusted gate) to frame the scene; the subject sits right of centre.
- Text lives in three places only: title lockup top-left (NPDO, 國有財產處置員, English line), tagline below it, and an optional lore blurb bottom right. The red word (處分) is the only colour accent.
- Props that recur and may become icons: the 國有財產署 現地勘查紀錄 clipboard form, ward signage with arrows, a floor directory (RF to B2), the green cross of the hospital sign.

## Assets in the project

- `menu-bg` (1672 x 941): intro key art, used by the main menu and the settings screen.
- `menu-title` (2172 x 724, alpha): game title logo.
- `hud-preview-bg` (1536 x 1024): in-game still used only as a HUD preview backdrop; not shipped art.

Art is darkened by the side and bottom shades so text stays above contrast; never place text on the bare image.
